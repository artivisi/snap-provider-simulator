Partner endpoint

For bank-to-partner calls: the bank requests a token from {base URL}/v1.0/access-token/b2b, then calls {base URL}/v1.0/transfer-va/inquiry and /payment. Client id and secret are the ones your app issued to the bank. BCA partners usually serve these under /openapi: include it in the base URL.

Test connection

The bank requests a token from {base URL}/v1.0/access-token/b2b, signed with the bank key.
